Install in 2 minutes

Four steps. No store, no waiting.

01

Download & unzip

Grab the extension zip and extract it. You'll get one folder called Extension — keep it somewhere it won't get deleted.

02

Load it in Chrome

Open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode, click “Load unpacked” and pick the Extension folder. The Avrel logo appears in your toolbar.

03

Connect with a code

Click the extension, choose “Get my login code”, copy the code from the Avrel tab that opens, paste it back. Your browser and dashboard are now paired.

Then just browse. Open a subreddit, a Facebook group or your LinkedIn feed and hit Start scan. Avrel checks the posts as you scroll — matches appear in your dashboard, scored, with drafts attached.

Straight talk

What it does.
What it doesn't.

It does

  • Read posts already visible on your screen
  • Score them against your keywords and intent signals
  • Draft replies in your voice, for your review
  • Run approved sequences under caps you set

It doesn't

  • Scrape anything behind a login you don't have
  • Send anything you haven't approved or scheduled
  • Touch your password — pairing is by one-time code
  • Pretend a lead is hot when it can't say why
