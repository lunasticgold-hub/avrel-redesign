How does the free trial work?

Seven days free: one platform, 50 saved-lead credits and 50 draft credits — enough to run the full loop from scan to reply. No sales call required to start, and if you later pay and see no revenue in 30 days, the guarantee has you covered anyway.

What is a lead credit?

One credit covers one lead saved to your dashboard (or one drafted outreach) in a month. Scanning and reading posts is unlimited — credits only count the leads you keep.

Can I change plans later?

Yes — upgrade when you need LinkedIn, more credits or more seats; downgrade when a project ends. Changes apply from the next billing cycle.

How do I claim the refund?

Email support within 60 days of your first payment, after at least 30 days of use, and say the words. We process it — no exit interview, no forms in triplicate.
